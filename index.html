Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about earning, claiming faucets, advertising, and withdrawals.

What is CryptoPulse and how does it work? +
CryptoPulse is an online rewards and advertising platform. Advertisers fund campaigns to promote their projects, and our registered members earn a share of those revenues by viewing ads, claiming from faucets, and completing surveys.
What is the minimum withdrawal amount and fee? +
Withdrawals to FaucetPay have a very low minimum of just $0.50 (500 Coins) and 0% transaction fee. Direct blockchain withdrawals to BTC, ETH, LTC, DOGE, TRX, and SOL have a $5.00 (5,000 Coins) minimum and a small network fee.
How does the Loyalty Streak bonus work? +
Every consecutive day you log in and make at least one faucet claim or check-in, your loyalty bonus increases by +1%, up to +100%. This doubles your base faucet rewards!
How fast do new advertising campaigns go live? +
All campaigns created through the Advertiser Portal are activated immediately inside the PTC Advertisements section for earners. When an earner views the ad, the remaining view count updates in real-time.
